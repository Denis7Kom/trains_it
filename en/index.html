01 / The essentials

Three concepts.
No confusion.

The service name on your ticket, the train model and your travel class answer different questions. A red train does not necessarily reach 300 km/h.

01
WHICH SERVICE?

Category

Frecciarossa, Intercity and Regionale describe the service: which towns it connects, where it stops and how tickets are sold. Italo is a separate high-speed operator.

02
WHICH TRAIN?

Rolling stock

ETR 1000, ETR 500, AGV, EVO and Rock are the physical trains. Their motors, cars, body shapes and maximum speeds differ. The model assigned to a particular service can change.

03
WHICH SEAT?

Travel class

Standard, Business and Executive, or Smart, Prima Business and Club Executive, are areas inside the train. The class affects your seat and onboard service. Refunds and changes also depend on the fare you choose.

Example: Frecciarossa 1000 · Business · Economy. Frecciarossa is the service, 1000 the model, Business the travel class and Economy the fare conditions. A higher class does not automatically make a ticket refundable.

04 / Beneath the bodywork

Why trains
differ so much.

The nose is only part of the story. The positions of motors, bogies and doors reflect the job a train was built to do.

ETR 500 / CONCENTRATED TRACTION

Power at the ends

Two power cars pull and push the passenger coaches between them. ETR 500 has 11 passenger coaches; its power cars are counted separately. A locomotive is a separate vehicle that provides traction.

A conventional Intercity often has one locomotive and a set of coaches, sometimes including a driving trailer. Capacity and equipment depend on the actual formation.

ETR 1000 / EVO / DISTRIBUTED TRACTION

Motors beneath the cars

An electric multiple unit combines its cars and traction equipment into one trainset. Motors are fitted to several bogies rather than a separate locomotive. Frecciarossa 1000 has 16 traction motors.

A bogie is a frame with wheelsets beneath the body. It supports the car and pivots on curves. More powered axles spread traction over more of the train.

AGV / ARTICULATED TRAINSET

A shared support

Adjacent Italo AGV cars share bogies, combined with distributed traction. An 11-car AGV has 12 bogies; a conventional arrangement would have two beneath each car.

EVO has a different architecture. Although derived from the Pendolino family, the Italian Italo EVO does not use active body tilting. The Pendolino name does not automatically mean that a train tilts.

ROCK / REGIONAL SERVICES

Capacity before records

Rock’s two decks provide many seats within a limited length. Wide entrances and brisk acceleration help on routes with frequent stops. Stairs lead to the upper deck; accessible areas are on the relevant entrance level.

Regional trains can also be single-deck: Pop, Jazz, Blues and other families. Electric, diesel and hybrid trains are all used. An R-category ticket does not promise a particular model.

300 km/h ≠ average speed. A hypothetical 600 km journey in 3 hours averages 200 km/h: 600 ÷ 3. That includes acceleration, braking and stops. Advertised design figures of 360–400 km/h do not mean your Italian service runs at that speed.

06 / Who gives way

The rails have no
“first class”.

The signaller manages traffic across the line. Priority depends on service type, time of day, the timetable and the consequences of delay.

TRAFFIC CONTROL / RFI PRINCIPLES

More than just
Frecciarossa → everyone else.

During weekday peaks, some commuter services around major cities share a management group with commercial long-distance trains. At night, faster freight trains can share a group with Intercity.

Frecciarossa does not take precedence over Italo simply because it belongs to Trenitalia. Both have their own agreed timetable paths.

Simplified groups from PIR 2026, June 2026 edition, §6.3.2. These principles cover normal traffic and minor disruption; they are not an unconditional departure order at every station. Freight V refers to the speed category used in the document. Technical services are omitted.

How overtaking works

Regional trainHigh-speed train

The slower train is routed into a station loop while the faster train passes on the main track. This is precedenza — letting a train travelling in the same direction pass. Opposing trains on a single-track line perform an incrocio — a crossing. They cannot pass each other in the middle of a single track.

TIMETABLE

A train path is a slot

The times at which a train occupies each section are agreed in advance. If services run to plan, overtakes are already built into the timetable. A stop alone is not evidence of a breakdown.

DURING DISRUPTION

The wider impact matters

Within the groups, control takes account of total delays, recovery of the timetable and major hubs. A train running early should not delay other categories.

FOR PASSENGERS

Colour does not decide priority

The logo and ticket price cannot predict a signaller’s decision. Major disruption requires additional measures. For connections, follow the actual delay.

08 / Practical travel advice

From booking
to your seat.

Many problems start with the wrong station, a ticket for another train or too little time to change trains.

Before boarding

0 / 6
Your ticks are saved only in this browser. Reset the list for a new journey.

Choose the right station

Roma Termini ≠ Tiburtina. Milano Centrale ≠ Porta Garibaldi. Firenze S. M. Novella ≠ Campo di Marte. Venezia S. Lucia is on the island; Mestre is on the mainland. Napoli Centrale ≠ Afragola.

Check the fare

Compare total journey time, changes, class and exchange/refund terms. Booked early, a high-speed ticket can cost less than Intercity. There is no universal fixed price.

Use the train number

Look for your train number and time on the Partenze departure board. The train’s final destination may be beyond yours. Binario means track; it may be assigned late or change before departure.

Board your coach

Carrozza means coach; posto means seat. Have your ticket and ID ready. Let passengers off before boarding. On an unreserved regional train, take a free seat in your class.

FRECCE / ITALO / INTERCITY

A ticket for a specific train

  • A coach and seat are normally assigned with the booking. Choosing a particular seat on the seating plan may cost extra.
  • An electronic Frecce / IC ticket does not need stamping. Save the QR code / PNR and keep the ticket accessible.
  • Italo has its own fares and rules. A Trenitalia ticket is not valid on Italo.
  • If you miss your train, do not automatically board the next one: check the change conditions of your fare first.

Travel class and fare flexibility are separate choices.

REGIONALE / REGIONALE VELOCE

Identify your ticket type first

  • BDR is Trenitalia’s Digital Regional Ticket. It validates automatically at the scheduled departure time. No manual check-in is needed. A valid identity document is required during inspection.
  • For an ordinary journey, the BDR is tied to the stated train. Change the time before its scheduled departure; different rules apply when the train is delayed or cancelled.
  • A paper ticket marked as requiring convalida must be stamped before boarding. Check its validity period and any exceptions on the ticket.
  • A seat number is not usually assigned. Buying a ticket does not guarantee that you will be seated throughout the journey.

BDR rules are not the rules for every ticket and every regional operator.

If all stamping machines are out of order: Trenitalia’s rules require you to approach the onboard staff as soon as you board. Do not wait for inspection. Resolve digital-ticket problems before departure; payment alone does not waive ticket requirements.
09 / Questions before you go

Things worth
knowing in advance.

These are practical limitations, not a promise of identical service on every train. Open the question you need.

Which class is worth choosing?

Frecciarossa: Standard is the basic option; Premium often keeps a 2+2 layout with upgraded finishes and service; Business usually offers 2+1 seating and more space; Executive is the most spacious area. Check the actual seating plan for your departure. Italo: Smart is the basic option, Prima Business adds space and service, and Club Executive is the top level. Coach numbers and facilities are not interchangeable between train models.

Can I sleep on Intercity Notte?

For sleep, book a berth or bed rather than simply a night-time departure. Comfort — couchette compartments; Relax — sleeping cars with cabins; Superior — a higher standard, where available. Easy is seated accommodation. Check occupancy, private or shared use, showers and toilets: these differ between coaches. A night train may save a hotel night, but compare the total cost of your trip.

What about a large suitcase?

Check the rules for your operator and class before paying. Use racks and luggage areas, not the neighbouring seat or aisle. Space for a large case does not mean a guaranteed individual locker. Keep documents and valuables with you. Double-deck trains have stairs; check accessible areas in advance if you have reduced mobility.

Are Wi-Fi and sockets available?

Frecciarossa and Italo advertise Wi-Fi, but continuous internet is not guaranteed: trains pass through tunnels and areas with different coverage. On regional trains, Wi-Fi and sockets depend on the rolling stock. There is insufficient reliable evidence of uninterrupted connectivity on every route for a critical work call. Save essential documents in advance and check your phone battery.

Can I take a bicycle?

Trenitalia allows one folded bicycle or a dismantled bicycle in a bag free of charge on Frecce. Its dimensions must not exceed 80 × 110 × 45 cm. A full-size assembled bicycle is a separate service: on suitable regional trains, check the bicycle symbol, space and supplement. Equipped Intercity trains have limited spaces that require reservation. Check Italo’s rules separately. Trenitalia conditions ↗

How do I arrange wheelchair travel?

Check the station, platform, wheelchair space and train equipment together. A low-floor train does not guarantee step-free, gap-free boarding at every platform. Contact RFI Sale Blu and the operator in advance. Confirm assistance at both ends and at connections. Booking lead times and availability depend on the station and contact method. Check assistance conditions ↗

How much time should I allow to change trains?

There is no universally safe interval. Changing platform levels, large stations, lifts, luggage and separate tickets increase the risk. For a first trip, 30–45 minutes is the author’s practical buffer, not an operator’s rule. Allow more before the final train of the day. Do not assume that a connection will be held for your arriving train.

What if my train is delayed?

Follow the operator’s information, station boards and staff announcements. For Trenitalia, use ViaggiaTreno ↗; for Italo, Italo in Viaggio ↗. If you miss a connection, speak to staff: your rights depend partly on whether you bought a through journey or separate transport contracts. Keep tickets and receipts. Before buying a replacement, check what is covered if you intend to seek reimbursement.

When is delay compensation available?

For Frecce, Intercity and Intercity Notte, Trenitalia states compensation of 25% of the fare for 60–119 minutes of delay at destination, and 50% for 120 minutes or more. A refund for abandoning a journey and compensation for delay are different procedures. Regional trains have further conditions and minimum amounts; Italo has its own claim process. Check exclusions and current rules. Official conditions ↗

What about strikes or engineering works?

Sciopero means a strike; lavori means engineering works. Check notices for your date and the lists of guaranteed trains: a protected time band does not guarantee every departure within it. There may be replacement buses — bus sostitutivo— with different boarding points and journey times. Allow for this before a flight or when carrying a bicycle.

How do I find a train to Sicily?

Check direct Intercity / Intercity Notte departures and journeys with connections. On some direct services from the mainland, passenger coaches cross the Strait of Messina by ferry; this is not a high-speed railway to the island. Trains may split into coach groups for different destinations, so check your coach and destination carefully.

What else should I check: children, pets, food?

Child-fare age limits and seating rules vary by operator and product. For pets, check carriers, muzzles, documents and supplements. A bistro or vending machines depend on the long-distance service; do not expect a restaurant car on Regionale. Bring suitable food if you have special dietary needs. Keep drinking water and medicines in your hand luggage.

10 / Station vocabulary

Eight terms
you will need.

Italian labels also appear in apps, announcements and station boards.

Binario
Track number. Binario 7 means track 7, not coach 7.
Partenze / Arrivi
Departures / arrivals. Use the Partenze board to find your train.
Carrozza / Posto
Coach / seat. For example, carrozza 4, posto 12A.
Ritardo
Delay. +20 usually means a delay of 20 minutes.
Convalida
Ticket validation. Paper tickets use a stamping machine; BDR validates automatically.
Soppresso
Train cancelled. Look for alternatives and ticket-change conditions.

Alta Velocità / Alta Capacità — high speed / high capacity.
